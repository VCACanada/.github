# 🐾 VCA Canada

Welcome to VCA Canada’s official GitHub!
We are a leading network of veterinary hospitals committed to compassionate care for pets and continuous innovation in animal health.

## 🌟 Who We Are

VCA Canada operates over 150 animal hospitals across the country, offering general, emergency, and specialty care. Our mission is to care for the future of veterinary medicine through clinical excellence, collaboration, and cutting-edge technology.

## 💻 What You’ll Find Here

This GitHub organization is home to resources developed by our IT and technical services teams to support hospital operations and veterinary care. Here you’ll find:

* Configuration guides and deployment documentation
* Technical standards and best practices
* Open-source tools and scripts used across our infrastructure
* Contributions to shared veterinary tech initiatives

We believe in knowledge sharing and operational transparency to elevate care across the veterinary ecosystem.
